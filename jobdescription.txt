Role Overview

Experience Range: 3–6 years of experience in production infrastructure, infrastructure engineering, Site Reliability Engineering, or DevOps roles.

Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or equivalent practical experience.

Role: Responsible for maintaining the reliability, availability, scalability, and performance of production systems and infrastructure.

Skills Breakdown

1. Minimum Skills (Must-Have Basics)

Core Linux/Unix system administration and command-line proficiency.

Basic scripting knowledge using Python, Bash, or Shell.

Understanding of networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, and load balancing.

Knowledge of troubleshooting production infrastructure and application issues.

Understanding of monitoring, logging, and alerting concepts.

Familiarity with Git and version control systems.

2. Cloud Infrastructure

Hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.

Understanding of compute, storage, networking, and identity management.

Experience with AWS EC2, S3, VPC, IAM, ELB, and Auto Scaling.

Knowledge of cloud infrastructure security and best practices.

Understanding of infrastructure provisioning and configuration management.

3. Containerization and Orchestration

Experience with Docker and container technologies.

Working knowledge of Kubernetes.

Understanding of Kubernetes deployments, services, pods, ConfigMaps, and Secrets.

Experience troubleshooting containerized applications.

Knowledge of Helm charts and Kubernetes resource management.

4. CI/CD and Automation

Experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Azure DevOps.

Understanding of automated build, testing, and deployment pipelines.

Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation.

Ability to automate operational tasks using Python or Shell scripting.

5. Monitoring and Observability

Experience with monitoring tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog, or Dynatrace.

Understanding of metrics, logs, and distributed tracing.

Knowledge of SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs.

Experience creating dashboards, alerts, and monitoring solutions.

Ability to identify performance bottlenecks and troubleshoot application issues.

6. Production Support and Incident Management

Experience handling production incidents and performing root cause analysis.

Knowledge of incident management and escalation processes.

Experience with problem management and preventive actions.

Understanding of high availability, disaster recovery, and fault tolerance.

Ability to participate in on-call support and production deployments.

Key Responsibilities

Maintain high availability, reliability, and performance of production infrastructure.

Monitor production systems and proactively identify potential failures.

Troubleshoot infrastructure, application, and network-related issues.

Develop automation scripts to reduce manual operational activities.

Implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines.

Manage cloud infrastructure and containerized workloads.

Build and maintain monitoring dashboards and alerting mechanisms.

Participate in incident response, root cause analysis, and post-incident reviews.

Implement reliability improvements and operational best practices.

Collaborate with development, security, and infrastructure teams.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience working in large-scale distributed systems.

Knowledge of microservices architecture.

Understanding of performance engineering and capacity planning.

Experience with GitOps tools such as ArgoCD.

Knowledge of security scanning and vulnerability management.

Familiarity with chaos engineering and resilience testing.

Understanding of Agile and DevOps methodologies.
